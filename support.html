Frequently Asked Questions

How do I record a promise?

On the Capture tab, type your promise in the text field or tap the microphone icon to record it with your voice. NightFuel will automatically transcribe what you say. Then do the same for your first step. Choose a reminder time and tap Save Promise.

When will I be reminded?

NightFuel sends you a notification at the time you choose when saving your promise. You can set a default reminder time in Settings, or customize it per promise.

What happens when I tap "Not yet"?

NightFuel will remind you again in 2 hours. If you tap "Not yet" a second time, the promise is marked as missed. You can always mark a missed promise as completed later from the Promises list.

Can I listen to my original recording?

Yes. If you recorded a promise with your voice, tap the promise in the Promises list to open it, then tap "Listen to original" to hear your recording.

Where is my data stored?

All data — text, recordings, and preferences — is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I delete a promise?

In the Promises tab, swipe left on any promise to delete it. To delete all data, go to Settings → Clear all data.

How do I export my promises?

Go to Settings → Export all promises. This creates a JSON file you can save or share however you like.

Transcription isn't working. What do I do?

Make sure you've granted NightFuel permission for both Microphone and Speech Recognition. You can check in iOS Settings → NightFuel. If transcription still fails, you can always type your promise manually — the text field is always available.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that notifications are enabled for NightFuel in iOS Settings → Notifications → NightFuel. Also make sure the Reminders toggle is on in NightFuel's Settings tab. If you're using Focus modes, ensure NightFuel is allowed through.

Can I change the reminder time after saving?

Tap the promise in the Promises list, then choose "Remind me later" to pick a new time.

App Requirements

  • iOS 17.0 or later
  • iPhone
  • Microphone access (optional, for voice recording)
  • Speech Recognition (optional, for transcription)
  • Notifications (recommended, for reminders)
